# Chapter 14: AI-style phrasing review

Reviewed and applied: 21 October 2026, on `opus5.5-readabilty` after commit `3f6f271`. These are stylistic flags, not evidence of AI authorship. The pattern matches the earlier chapter reviews: dramatic setup, neat contrast, quotable takeaway, self-reference and intensifiers. Several flags are in passages added to Chapter 14 on the same day.

Kept deliberately: the book-wide terms “ceiling”, “operational definition of relevance” and the three-controls formula; the pull quotes “A metric does not measure relevance…”, “Quoting a metric is choosing a definition of better…” (also quoted in `tools/chapter-14-videos/episodes.json`) and “A benchmark result is evidence about a benchmark…”; the MRR “too far down” line; and teaching contrasts that carry a distinction.
