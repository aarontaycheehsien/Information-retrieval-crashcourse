Recorded 9 October 2026 while revising the chapter from commit `acb3b22`.

The author clarified that the Semantic Scholar comparison demonstrates poorer
search quality for the natural-language question, as well as fewer results.
The stated basis is the known literature on the open-access citation advantage
(dozens to hundreds of relevant studies) and inspection of the leading 10–20
results, where available. The author requested a concise statement without
paper-by-paper examples.

The existing August 2026 screenshots separately document 13 results for the
question and about 35,300 for the keyword query. The author’s qualitative
assessment is recorded here as the evidence for the quality judgement. The
inspection date, individual relevance judgements and exact inspection depth
for each query were not supplied. This revision did not independently rerun
or regrade the searches, and reports no measured precision or recall.

The poorer performance in this example does not establish the query parser,
the exact cause, or a general advantage for keyword queries.
