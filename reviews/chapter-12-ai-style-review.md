# Chapter 12: AI-style phrasing review

Reviewed against the [chapter 12 source on `fable-1`](https://github.com/aarontaycheehsien/Information-retrieval-crashcourse/blob/fable-1/search-textbook.html#L2714). These are stylistic flags, not evidence of AI authorship. The pattern is repeated dramatic setup, neat contrast, and quotable takeaway. The chapter’s technical distinctions often need contrast; these examples stand out because of their presentation or repetition.

The boldest flags are the “worth sitting with” setup, “The cell exists, and it is occupied,” “Three further difficulties…” suspense line, “None of this is an argument against…,” and “Nothing at all — and that is the point.” The repeated cadence across the chapter is more notable than any one sentence.
