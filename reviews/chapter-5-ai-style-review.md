The most noticeable pattern is the accumulation of coaching and significance cues: “carry forward,” “worth holding on to,” “the whole move,” “the part that matters here,” and “the other half of the account.” Individually, some are ordinary teaching prose; together, they make the chapter sound heavily AI-shaped.
