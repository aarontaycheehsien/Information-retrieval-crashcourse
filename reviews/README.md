# Editorial records — not the published book

This directory preserves review findings, readability plans and editorial
ledgers. They describe particular snapshots: a criticism may already be fixed,
and a proposed change is not necessarily an outstanding task or an approved
instruction. The maintained edition is [search-textbook.html](../search-textbook.html).

## Reading the records

- **Readability:** [plan](readability-plan.md), [Part I](part-i-readability-review.md),
  [Part II](part-ii-readability-review.md), [Part III](part-iii-readability-review.md),
  and the [repetition ledger](repetition-ledger.md).
- **Content and consistency:** [Chapters 5–11](chapter-5-11-review.md),
  [Part III review](part-iii-review.md), [second Part III review](part-iii-review-2.md),
  [appendices, labs and Part III](appendices-labs-part-iii-review.md),
  [teaching and labs](teaching-and-labs-review.md), and
  [whole-book consistency](whole-book-consistency-review.md).
- **Completed restructuring:** [phase records, snapshots and historical scripts](restructuring/README.md).

The records were relocated without changing their contents. Filenames, line
numbers, counts and paths inside them refer to their original snapshots, often
when these files were at the repository root. Consult Git history before using
them to make changes. The restructuring gate record is historical, not a live
approval queue.

Current release history is [CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md); current maintenance
instructions are [tools/README.md](../tools/README.md). New review records belong
here and should identify their date and the commit or edition reviewed.

This is an organizational archive, not a privacy boundary. Its files remain in
the repository and may be served by the static site host.
