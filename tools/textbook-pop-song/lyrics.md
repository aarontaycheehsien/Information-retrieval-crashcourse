# Follow the Evidence

Original synth-pop companion to Aaron Tay’s *How Search Decides What You See*.
120 BPM · C major · 4/4 · 2:58.

**Verse 1**

You type a question, hit the light\
A thousand stars come into sight\
But somewhere past the page you see\
A missing paper waits quietly

**Pre-chorus**

Behind the screen, a story starts\
Let’s learn about the moving parts

**Chorus**

Follow, follow the evidence\
Past the glow upon the screen\
How search decides what you see\
There’s a world behind the machine

Follow, follow the evidence\
Let the questions lead the way\
Turn the page and trace the signal\
Learn a little more today

**Verse 2**

Some words can open, some can close\
Some scores decide which paper shows\
From Boolean gates to vector maps\
Learn where the evidence slips through the gaps

*Repeat pre-chorus and chorus.*

**Bridge**

A real citation tells one story\
It can’t tell you what was missed\
Look inside the working set\
There’s more than what made the list

*Repeat chorus with fuller harmonies.*

**Outro**

Turn the page and trace the signal\
Learn a little more today

The lyrics use the textbook’s retrieval themes as pop-song metaphors. They do
not promise that every missing paper can be recovered. All lyric wording,
melody and instrumental parts are original. The demo uses an explicitly
synthetic electronic singing voice.
