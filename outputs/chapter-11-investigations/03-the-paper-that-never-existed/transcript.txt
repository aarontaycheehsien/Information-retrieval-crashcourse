[00:00:23.794]: It can serve as a retrieval aid. Query2doc and HyDE use generated language in different ways to find real indexed sources. Neither method turns the imagined paragraph into evidence, or establishes that the described study exists.

[00:00:40.750]: Query2doc generates a pseudo document and uses its language to expand the original query. The additional vocabulary may resemble wording found in relevant documents. The expanded input can go to a sparse retriever such as BM twenty five.

[00:00:58.417]: Dense retrieval is also possible; the diagram follows the lexical route. Its effect depends on what the next retriever does with those words.

[00:01:08.212]: HyDE means Hypothetical Document Embeddings. An LLM generates a hypothetical relevant document, then a dense encoder embeds that document. Retrieval compares the resulting vector with stored vectors for real indexed documents.

[00:01:25.044]: The generated document is a pivot into the vector space. It is not the final answer or a verified source. Unlike Query2doc’s language expansion, this path uses the generated document’s embedding to conduct the search.

[00:01:40.536]: The chapter’s illustrative paragraph mentions comparisons and adjustment for discipline and year. Those details may steer retrieval towards particular vocabulary or study designs. They are not findings about an identified paper, and may import assumptions the searcher did not intend.

[00:01:59.807]: Retrieving real sources does not retroactively make every generated detail true. Read and assess the actual sources, and retain the generated text in the search record. It is part of the retrieval strategy, not supporting evidence.

[00:02:16.728]: The chapter’s product accounts describe different receivers. EBSCOhost and EDS AI Assisted Search parse keyword and noun phrases for the established engine; the cited public page does not identify the parsing model as an LLM.

[00:03:15.832]: That Research Assistant’s guidance also lists complex field tagged Boolean queries as unsupported. Supported natural language patterns do not replace the database’s complete advanced search language. Keep product features distinct and inspect the actual generated conditions.

[00:03:35.296]: A filter inferred from the question is different from one the user selects explicitly. Primo Research Assistant documents limited automatic refinements; manually changing a refinement switches that field to manual handling, so it is no longer inferred automatically.
