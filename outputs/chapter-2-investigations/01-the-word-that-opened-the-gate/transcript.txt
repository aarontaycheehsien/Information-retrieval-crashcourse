[00:00:16.654]: In a formal Boolean strategy, an OR alternative can open another route into the eligible set. The new word does not merely receive a low weight. It changes an admission rule.

[00:00:45.577]: This is an intersection: keep identifiers shared by the sets. D two and D three satisfy job but fail the delulu condition. Strict Boolean retrieval has membership, not almost-membership.

[00:01:14.526]: OR includes records satisfying either condition, including both. Adding an alternative can widen eligibility. It does not, by itself, establish whether the extra records help with your information need.
