## Application exercise II — Map a product onto the pipeline

How much of a product’s retrieval pipeline can you establish from the available evidence?

1. Choose an institutional or publicly accessible product that advertises semantic, natural-language or AI search.
2. Use technical documentation, release notes and help pages to map the six stages: query transformation, analysis and execution rules, first-stage retrieval, fusion, reranking and presentation.
   Note any routing or feedback decision.
   Then add the behavioural probe below as a separate evidence source.
   Marketing claims alone do not establish an architecture.
3. Mark every stage as **documented** (the vendor states it), **inferred** (you deduced it from observed behaviour), or **unknown**.
   Use **documented: not used** when documentation explicitly states a stage is absent.
4. For the indexed unit, develop and test a hypothesis about whether the product retrieves titles, abstracts, passages or full text.
   Search for a claim known to appear only in a paper’s full text, then record what the result supports, what alternative explanations remain and what only vendor documentation could establish.

::: {.exercise-deliverable}
**Deliverable.** A completed six-stage map with each stage labelled by evidence type, plus the single question you would put to the vendor about the stage you could least determine.
Bring the map to your next renewal conversation.
:::
