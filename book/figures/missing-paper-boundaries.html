The same known relevant paper P is missing from the displayed results in each hypothetical case. These panels are alternative explanations to investigate.

1 · Outside the collection

P: outside the searchable collection

The source was never covered, or indexing has not caught up.

Check coverage and indexing lag.

2 · Excluded by a filter

P: in the collection → blocked by a filter

A date or format condition excludes the paper.

Check the applied limits and metadata.

3 · Absent from the candidate pool

P: eligible → not in the shortlist

The retrieval stage did not supply the paper to later stages.

Investigate the query, indexed evidence, retrieval method and candidate cut-off.

4 · Retrieved but not displayed

P: in the candidate pool → below the display cut-off

The paper reached the shortlist but not the visible results.

Inspect ranking, later selection and presentation limits.

Unknown without documentation or a trace: eligibility and internal candidate membership may not be visible. A failed lookup alone does not prove which boundary excluded P.
