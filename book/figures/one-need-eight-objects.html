Controlled term or structured expressionUse the database’s own subject heading for open-access publishing, and tag the phrase to title and abstract fields.
Citation edgeStart from one key study and follow what cites it, and what it cited. Reaches work whose wording you could not have guessed.
Seed documentHand a known review to a query-by-example route such as PubMed’s Similar Articles, and let the paper stand for the question.
Relevance judgementMark the five most useful records from a first pass, and let feedback build the next query out of them.
Browse or monitoring targetSet an alert on the topic, or work the journals where this literature actually appears.
Programmatic predicateAsk the OpenAlex API for works matching the concept, filtered by the year range in the information need, so the request can be rerun and cited.
Task briefState the scope, the inclusion and exclusion criteria, which sources to cover, when to stop and what to record—then let a controller generate the queries.
